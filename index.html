Features

One-click reply

Open an email, click Reply with Giraffe, and get a draft that fits the thread.

Rewrite anything

Select your text and make it shorter, clearer, friendlier, or more formal.

Your tone

Set a few sample sentences once. Drafts follow how you actually write.

You stay in control

Nothing is sent for you. Every draft lands in the compose box for review.

How it works

1. Install

Add the extension to Chrome and open Gmail.

2. Click

Use the Giraffe button next to Reply or inside compose.

3. Edit & send

Adjust the draft if needed, then send as usual.
